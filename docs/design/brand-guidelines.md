# Brand Guidelines — Amazon Re-imagined

## 1. Brand Essence & Voice
- **Core Personality**: Authoritative, frictionless, transparent, ultra-modern.
- **Voice**: Direct, factual, helpful, zero-nonsense. No marketing fluff or misleading countdown gimmicks.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`
- **Scale Hierarchy**:
  - `Hero Display`: 32px / 2rem, 700 bold
  - `Page Section Title`: 22px / 1.375rem, 700 bold
  - `Product Card Title`: 15px / 0.9375rem, 500 medium, line-clamp-2
  - `Body Copy`: 14px / 0.875rem, 400 regular
  - `Metadata & Tags`: 12px / 0.75rem, 500 medium

## 4. Logo Usage
- The Amazon Re-imagined vector logo consists of the crisp bold wordmark paired with the iconic curved arrow smile transitioning from **A to Z**, accented with an electric cyan "ULTRA" badge.
