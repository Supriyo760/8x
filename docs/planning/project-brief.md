# Project Brief — Amazon Re-imagined (24h Engineering Sprint)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Project Name**: Amazon Re-imagined ("Amazon Ultra")
- **Core Mission**: Rebuild Amazon.com's core retail experience in a 24-hour engineering window, not as a dated clone, but as a superior, high-signal commerce platform that eliminates cognitive fatigue and ad clutter while preserving Amazon's core trust, speed, and selection.
- **Target Audience**: Modern e-commerce consumers who value fast discovery, transparent pricing, verified review truth, and frictionless purchasing.

## 2. Why We Are Building This (The Problem Statement)
Amazon.com is the world's most dominant retail marketplace, yet it suffers from acute 15-year-old UX debt:
1. **Ad Overload & Sponsored Pollution**: Organic search results are buried under 4+ rows of sponsored placements.
2. **Review Fatigue & Bot Noise**: Customers must sift through thousands of spam or low-effort reviews to find genuine quality indicators.
3. **Multi-Tab Comparison Chaos**: Comparing 3–4 items requires opening multiple browser tabs, juggling contradictory spec sheets, and manually tracking price differentials.
4. **Disjointed Discovery Flow**: Navigating to a full page just to check variant inventory breaks the user's shopping momentum.

## 3. Product Vision & Value Proposition
"Amazon Re-imagined" delivers a **10x cleaner, faster, and more transparent** shopping experience:
- **Zero-Latency Omni-Search**: Instant faceted filtering by price, brand, rating, and Prime status with active chip management.
- **AI Review Synthesis ("The Verdict")**: Pre-computed executive summaries capturing consensus pros, honest cons, and buyer profiles.
- **Floating 1-Click Comparison Dock**: Side-by-side spec differential matrix for 2–4 products without leaving the catalog.
- **Quick-Peek Slide Drawer**: Inspect multi-angle imagery, variants, and stock without losing search scroll position.
- **Transparent Speed Buy Box**: Live countdown timer for same-day/next-day Prime delivery with clear return rules.
- **Streamlined 2-Step Accordion Checkout**: Distraction-free, zero-upsell checkout with real-time order tracking simulation.

## 4. Key Success Metrics & Criteria
- **Lighthouse Performance Score**: > 90 across Performance, Accessibility, and Best Practices.
- **Interaction to Next Paint (INP)**: < 50ms across all filter and variant changes.
- **End-to-End Flow Completion**: 100% functional flow from home discovery → search → PDP → cart → checkout → tracking.
- **Independence & Public Access**: Zero server-side login barriers; opens instantly for any external evaluator.
